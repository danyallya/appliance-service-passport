# Product brief

Problem: Connect a device QR to model, compatible parts, manuals and repair history.

Audience: appliance owners and repair technicians

## First release

One user role, one core record (devices) and one reversible workflow. Delay integrations and notifications until a backlog item justifies them.

## Guardrail

Publish only owner-approved data; verify manual and part sources.

Measure task completion, correction rate, offline recovery, trust, retention and deletion only after purpose and consent are documented.
